Phase 3 · Typography

Bilingual type scale

Sinhala resolves to the bundled Noto Sans Sinhala; Latin to the system sans (Roboto Flex on Android 16). Line heights are tuned for Sinhala ascenders and the ZWJ conjuncts (ශ්‍රී = ශ + ් + ZWJ + ර + ී).
